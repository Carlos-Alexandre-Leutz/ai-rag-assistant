Vermilion Labs Employee Handbook

Welcome. Vermilion Labs was founded in 2019 in Porto Alegre, Brazil, and today has 84 employees across three offices: Porto Alegre, Lisbon and Austin.

Working hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in each employee's local time. Outside core hours people may arrange their own schedules with their team lead. Remote work is allowed up to three days per week. Employees who want to work remotely from another country for more than 30 days per year need approval from the People team.

Equipment. Every new hire receives a laptop within the first week. The standard laptop is a 14-inch ultralight model with 32 GB of memory. A monitor stipend of 400 euros is available once per employee. Broken equipment must be reported to the IT desk at it-desk@vermilionlabs.example within 48 hours.

Onboarding. New hires follow a four-week onboarding plan. In week one they meet their buddy, who is a colleague from another team. In week two they ship a small change to production with a mentor. The probation period lasts 90 days, and feedback sessions happen at day 30, day 60 and day 90.

Offices. The Porto Alegre office is the headquarters and is open from 08:00 to 19:00. The Austin office has a quiet room and a phone booth for calls. The Lisbon office has a rooftop terrace that can be booked for team events up to twice per quarter.

Public holidays. Employees follow the public holiday calendar of the country where their employment contract is based. If a holiday falls on a weekend it is not moved to another day.

Vacation. Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and carried days expire on March 31. Vacation requests of more than 10 consecutive days must be submitted at least 6 weeks in advance.

Sick leave. Employees can take up to 12 paid sick days per year without a medical certificate. From the 13th day a certificate is required. Parental leave is 20 weeks for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the other parent.

Learning budget. Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, books and conferences. Conference travel is paid from a separate travel budget and must be approved by the team lead. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

Health. The company pays 80 percent of the premium for the health plan of the employee. Dental coverage is optional and paid fully by the employee.

Performance reviews. Reviews happen twice a year, in June and in December. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from three peers and a conversation with the team lead. Salary adjustments are decided once a year, in January.

Remote allowance. Employees who work remotely at least three days a week receive a home-office allowance of 35 euros per month, paid together with the salary.

Referral program. An employee who refers a candidate that is hired and stays for six months receives a bonus of 1,000 euros.

Wellbeing. The company offers 6 free sessions per year with an external psychologist. Sessions are confidential and the company never receives information about who used them.

Security. Passwords must have at least 14 characters and be stored in the company password manager. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts. Lost devices must be reported to the security team within 2 hours, using the hotline +351 210 000 000.

Expenses. Expenses up to 50 euros do not need prior approval. Expenses above 500 euros need approval from a director. Receipts must be uploaded to the expense tool within 15 days; late receipts are not reimbursed.

Office rules. The Lisbon office closes at 20:00. Visitors must register at reception and wear a badge. Pets are not allowed in any office.

Contacts. Questions about this handbook can be sent to people@vermilionlabs.example.

Data protection. Customer data may only be accessed through the approved admin tool, and every access is logged. Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or sent by email.

Incident response. Security incidents are classified from level 1 (minor) to level 4 (critical). Level 4 incidents must be escalated to the on-call director immediately and a post-mortem is published within 5 working days.

Travel. Flights are booked through the travel tool, in economy class for trips shorter than 6 hours. Hotels are reimbursed up to 140 euros per night in Europe and 180 dollars per night in the United States.

Code of conduct. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. Reports can be made anonymously through the ethics line, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith leads to disciplinary action.
